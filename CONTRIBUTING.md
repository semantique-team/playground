# Contributing

The playground is public as a showcase and for reuse under the license, but it is
**not open to external contributions**. Issues and pull requests from outside the
Semantique Team are not accepted: issues are disabled and pull requests opened by
anyone else are closed automatically, without review.

This repository is developed exclusively by the Semantique Team.

If you find a problem in the models themselves, the right places are the model
repositories, which do take reports and contributions:

- [ant-4b](https://github.com/semantique-team/ant-4b)
- [ant-08b](https://github.com/semantique-team/ant-08b)
